- Favorita now runs on OneConfig and needs it installed. All of its settings are in the OneConfig menu.
- The favorite slot key is a OneConfig keybind. It still shows up in Minecraft's Controls menu, and it can be a mouse button or a key combination.
- Each singleplayer world now has its own locked slots. Before, every world shared one set. A world you play for the first time after updating starts with that old shared set.
- Locked slots are now kept safe from number key and offhand swaps, and from double-clicks that gather matching items. The double-click protection can be turned off.
- Shift-clicking and dragging stacks skip locked slots, so items go to the next free slot instead.
- You can stop the favorite slot key from unlocking your held hotbar slot while no inventory is open, so slots only unlock from an inventory.
- You can pick the lock's tint color, hide the padlock, and choose whether hotbar locks always show, show for a few seconds when used, or never show.
- Sounds can be turned off, and the locked slot warning can be a notification, a chat message or nothing.
- Added `/favorita` to open the settings and `/favorita clear` to unlock every slot in the current world or server.
- Locked slots move from `Deftu/favorita.json` to `config/Favorita/favorites.json` on first launch.
- Supports Minecraft 1.21 to 1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.5, 1.21.8, 1.21.10, 1.21.11 and 26.1 to 26.3 on Fabric, and 1.8.9 on Ornithe. Forge, NeoForge and Minecraft 1.12.2 to 1.20.6 are no longer supported.
